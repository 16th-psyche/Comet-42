**Comet 42 1.1 makes it faster to get from "I need to fix this" to fixed.** You can capture part
of the screen without a screenshot first, search presets by typing `/`, run any preset from
anywhere with its own shortcut, and edit an answer before it goes back into your document. A
welcome guide now walks new users through setup.

## ✨ What's new

- **Capture part of the screen.** Press **Capture** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>) and
  drag over anything on screen. It's attached to your question. No more <kbd>⌃⇧⌘4</kbd> first.
- **Preset search.** Type <kbd>/</kbd> and a few letters in the question box. <kbd>↑</kbd>
  <kbd>↓</kbd> choose, <kbd>↩</kbd> runs. A pinned **Presets** menu also lists every preset, so
  none are hidden past the edge of the panel.
- **Shortcuts for presets.** Give any preset its own global shortcut in **Settings → Presets**.
  Select text in any app and press it. Presets set to *Replace selection directly* paste the
  result in place without opening the panel.
- **Edit before you paste.** **Edit** lets you tweak an answer, and **Insert After**
  (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>↩</kbd>) keeps your original text and adds the answer below it.
- **Chat history (opt-in).** Turn on **Keep chat history** in **Settings → General** to keep your
  last 50 chats on this Mac. Reopen them with **History** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Y</kbd>).
- **Codex streams word by word** and lists your account's models automatically. If your Codex
  version can't do this, Comet 42 falls back to the previous method on its own.
- **Welcome guide.** First launch walks you through your AI tools, the Accessibility permission
  and a shortcut that doesn't clash with other apps. Open it again any time from the menu-bar
  menu (**Welcome…**).
- **Open at login**, in **Settings → General**.
- **Update notices.** Comet 42 checks GitHub once a day and shows **Update Available** in the
  menu-bar menu and **Settings → About**. You can turn this off.

## 🛠 Improvements

- **Lower battery use when idle.** Background wake-ups drop by about two-thirds (from 1.2 to 0.4
  a second in our measurement), and the permission check stops once access is granted.
- **Settings** gains Startup and History sections, and a live update status on the About page.
- **Releases are built automatically by GitHub Actions** and signed with the same certificate as
  1.0, so the Accessibility permission carries over when you update.

## ⬆️ Updating from 1.0

1. Quit Comet 42 (menu-bar comet → **Quit Comet 42**).
2. Download **`Comet-42-1.1.0-macOS-universal.dmg`** below and drag **Comet 42** into
   **Applications**, replacing the old copy.
3. Open it. Your settings, presets and Accessibility permission are kept.

Version 1.0 has no update checker, so this is the last update you'll need to find by hand.

## 📦 New install

1. Download the DMG below, open it, and drag **Comet 42** into **Applications**.
2. Open it, and follow the welcome guide.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Comet 42 isn't notarized by Apple yet,** so the first time you open it, macOS warns that it
> can't verify the app and won't open it. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and
> click **Open Anyway** next to Comet 42, or run
> `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Comet 42.app"`.

**Requirements:** macOS 26 or later, and the
[Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/setup) or [Codex](https://developers.openai.com/codex/cli)
CLI signed in. **Capture** asks for Screen Recording permission the first time you use it.

## ⚠️ Known limitations

- **Not notarized** (see above).
- **Insert After** moves the cursor to the end of your selection with the → key. A few apps where
  → does something else may put the answer in the wrong place; use **Copy** there.
- **Codex streaming is new** and has had less real-world testing than Claude. If it can't start,
  Comet 42 falls back to the 1.0 method; if anything looks off, please
  [open an issue](https://github.com/16th-psyche/Comet-42/issues).
