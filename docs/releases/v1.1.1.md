<!-- title: Comet 42 1.1.1 — no more alert sound, instant panel -->
**A quick fix release.** Pressing the shortcut with nothing selected no longer plays the macOS
alert sound, the panel opens instantly, and ⌘W now closes windows.

## 🐞 Fixes

- **No more alert sound.** When nothing was selected, Comet 42 tried to copy the selection with
  ⌘C, and apps whose Copy command was greyed out played the alert sound. Comet 42 now checks
  whether Copy is available first, and sends nothing if it isn't.
- **The panel opens instantly.** It no longer waits for you to release the shortcut's keys, or for
  a copy that never comes. It opens straight away and shows "Reading selection…" while your
  selected text loads. If you type a question and press <kbd>↩</kbd> before it arrives,
  Comet 42 waits for it, so the selection is never left out.
- **⌘W closes windows.** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd> closes Settings and the welcome window, and
  hides the panel. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> minimizes Settings.
- **Preset shortcuts** get the same fixes: silent with nothing selected, and faster to start.

## ⬆️ Updating

Comet 42 1.1 shows **Update Available** in its menu-bar menu. Click it, download
**`Comet-42-1.1.1-macOS-universal.dmg`** below, quit Comet 42, and drag the new copy into
**Applications**. Your settings, presets and Accessibility permission are kept.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **New install?** Comet 42 isn't notarized by Apple yet. The first time you open it, go to
> **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**, or run
> `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Comet 42.app"`.

Requires macOS 26 or later and the Claude Code or Codex CLI.
